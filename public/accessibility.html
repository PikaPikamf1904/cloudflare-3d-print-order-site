Accessibility

The shop is designed for keyboard, touch, and screen-reader use. It includes visible focus indicators, large controls, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts, and light/dark themes.

Need help?

If any part of the store is difficult to use, create a problem report and share it with the enrichment program administrator. Include the page and what you were trying to do, but do not include passwords or payment credentials.
