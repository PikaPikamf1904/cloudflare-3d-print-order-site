LEGAL & INFORMATION

Privacy

The shop collects the first name, class, email address, order choices, and optional notes submitted with an order. This information is used to prepare, deliver, and manage that order.

Public receipts

A receipt link may show the order number, first name, class, items, locked prices, total, and order/payment status. It does not show the customer email, private administrator notes, or admin activity history.

Administration

Order management is protected by a private administrator password. Payment-provider secrets are not stored in page code or returned by public APIs.

Questions

Ask the enrichment program administrator if you need an order corrected or want to understand how order information is used.
